## DeepFilterNet (https://github.com/Rikorose/DeepFilterNet)

Rikorose/DeepFilterNet@d375b2d8309e0935d165700c91da9de862a99c31), and `DeepFilterNet3Model.swift` ports the signal
processing of its `libDF` crate. DeepFilterNet is licensed under either of the Apache License, Version 2.0, or the
